- **Facility:** MGM National Harbor casino/resort, 101 MGM National Ave

- **Electrical mechanism:** fountain
- **Verification level:** **VERIFIED**
- **How we found it:** discovery_source = `LOA1`
- **Independent source count:** 6
- **Primary sources:**
  - news: Washington Post — https://www.washingtonpost.com/local/public-safety/girl-electrocuted-at-mgm-national-harbor/
  - news: NBC Washington — https://www.nbcwashington.com/news/local/girl-7-injured-at-mgm-national-harbor/
  - legal: WTOP — https://wtop.com/prince-georges-county/2019/12/family-of-girl-injured-at-mgm-national-harbor-reaches-settlement/
  - news: FOX5 DC
  - news: NBC News
  - news: Patch

**Summary:** On June 26, 2018, 6-year-old Zynae Green was severely injured by electric shock at an outdoor decorative fountain area (Potomac Plaza) at MGM National Harbor casino/resort in Oxon Hill, Maryland. The electrical source was an illuminated metal handrail near the fountain. An LED driver designed to step 120V down to 12V had failed due to improper installation and faulty wiring, causing full 120V AC to energize the metal handrail. Zynae suffered massive traumatic brain injury and cardiac arrest, and was left paralyzed, unable to walk or talk — a severe permanent disability. Her 5-year-old brother, 16-year-old sister (hospitalized several days), mother Rydricka Rosier, and an MGM security guard were also injured in the incident. Critically, surveillance video reviewed after the incident showed other patrons being jolted by the same handrail in the days BEFORE Zynae's injury, meaning the hazard was observable and the prior shock events were not acted upon. A settlement was reached in December 2019; MGM and contractors paid an undisclosed amount.

**Open / research notes:** Found via Phase 3 broad search campaign (April 2026) via LOA1 news archive search. Significant case for the 'prior knowledge ignored' pattern — surveillance video documented shocks in the days before the severe incident, closely paralleling the Jupiter FL Harbourside Place fountain incident (ESD-2023-10-22-1, Davenport) where 15+ people were shocked three months before the fatal event. This is a distinct hazard subcategory from typical ESD: the electrical source was an illuminated metal handrail adjacent to (not within) the fountain, and the failure mode was an LED driver stepping 120V→12V that failed and passed line voltage through to the metal rail. Classified as fountain_electrocution because it occurred in a decorative fountain plaza context, but the electrical source detail should be preserved for hazard-category analysis. Non-fatal but with severe permanent disability — important case for injury-outcome studies, not just mortality. Casino/resort venue is notable as a commercial hospitality setting with multiple victims including a responding security guard.
