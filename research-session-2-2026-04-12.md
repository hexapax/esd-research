### 3. 50-State Regulatory Landscape

**States with dock-specific ESD laws (3):**
- Tennessee: Noah Dean and Nate Act (2014) — EXCLUDES private residential docks
- West Virginia: Michael Cunningham Act (2013) — EXCLUDES private residential docks
- Arkansas: Jesse's Law (2011) — scope ambiguous, enforcement focused on commercial

**Pending legislation (2):**
- Pennsylvania: Costa/Bartolotta bill (reintroduced June 2025, 16 co-sponsors) — WOULD cover residential
- South Carolina: H.3070 introduced 2021 — DID NOT PASS, not confirmed reintroduced

**NEC adoption:**
- 25 states on 2023 NEC (residential docks covered by expanded Article 555)
- 15 states on 2020 NEC
- 3 states on 2017 NEC
- 2 states on 2008 NEC (Kansas, Indiana commercial) — residential docks NOT covered
- 4 states with NO statewide NEC (Arizona, Illinois, Mississippi, Missouri)

**The Three Gaps:**
1. Residential Dock Gap: Even states with ESD laws exclude private residential docks
2. Existing Dock Gap: No state mandates retroactive inspection
3. Sale/Transfer Gap: Only 3 jurisdictions require dock electrical review at property sale (OK-GRDA, VA-SML, GA-Lanier via Corps)

**Worst-risk states:** Alabama (no license needed to electrify a dock), Kansas/Illinois (2008 NEC), Arizona/Mississippi/Missouri (no statewide NEC)

**Best coverage:** Federal property managers (USACE, TVA) — not state law
