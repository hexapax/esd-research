# storm-2024-05-28-1: Downed Power Line Electrocutes Three at Lake Conroe Campground — Montgomery County TX

## Summary

During severe thunderstorms on May 28, 2024, a power line came down at Lake Conroe Campgrounds (Lakeside RV campground) off FM 830 in Montgomery County, Texas. A 59-year-old man was found by neighbors still in contact with the line, severely burned and reported "on fire"; he died June 5, 2024 from his injuries. Charlotte Winters (56) and her six-year-old grandson Nathan Winters — who were "far away" from the line — received severe burns from ground current traveling through wet, saturated soil. Charlotte sustained 2nd and 3rd-degree burns on 55% of her body and was facing amputations; Nathan sustained 2nd-degree burns on 18% of his body. Both were admitted to ICU.

## Why Out of Scope

This incident is stored in the out-of-scope dataset because the electrical source was a storm-downed utility power line, not dock or marina wiring regulated under NEC 555. However, it follows exactly the same physical mechanism as Electric Shock Drowning: current propagating through a wet, conductive medium and injuring people who never made direct contact with the energized conductor. It is a key case for the storm electrocution surveillance gap thesis, demonstrating that ground current through saturated soil can cause severe, even fatal, injuries at distance from the source.
