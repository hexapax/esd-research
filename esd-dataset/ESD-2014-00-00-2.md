# ESD-2014-00-00-2: Three Swimmers Shocked at O'Connor Pool Ladder — Philadelphia, PA

## Summary

During Summer 2014, three swimmers were shocked while climbing the ladder at O'Connor Pool, a public city pool at 26th and South Streets in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Electrical current was flowing through the pool ladder, though the specific cause was not disclosed in news reports. All three victims were treated and released from the hospital, and the pool was closed for the season after the incident. Victim names were not published. This is one of the few documented public city pool ESD incidents in the dataset.
