# ESD-2016-05-28-2: Unknown 27-year-old man — Phoenix, AZ

## Summary

On May 28, 2016, an unknown 27-year-old father was electrocuted in the deep end of his private backyard swimming pool near 43rd Avenue and Thomas Road in Phoenix, Arizona, reportedly while attempting to change a faulty pool light. His wife and three children were all shocked when they got close to him attempting to rescue him — a classic rescuer cascade. The victim died from his injuries; the four family members survived the shocks. The home likely lacked GFCI protection (older residence), and the victim's name was not released in publicly available sources.
