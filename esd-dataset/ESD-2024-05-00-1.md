# ESD-2024-05-00-1: Heather Paquette and daughter — Edgewater Resort & Waterpark, Duluth, MN

## Summary

In May 2024, Heather Paquette (of Superior, WI) and her juvenile daughter were injured at the Edgewater Resort & Waterpark in Duluth, Minnesota when the metal gate knob/handle at the outside whirlpool/spa area was electrified. Heather reached to open the gate and received a shock so strong she could not release the knob; her daughter was also shocked while attempting to pull her mother's hand free. A bystander broke the circuit by pushing Heather to the ground. Heather was hospitalized and her daughter was treated and released. The incident is distinct from the classic dock/pool ESD pattern in that the energized point was the gate handle rather than the water itself, and it is one of very few documented Minnesota ESD-related events, a state that is severely underrepresented in the dataset.
