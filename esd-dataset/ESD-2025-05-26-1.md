# ESD-2025-05-26-1: Electric Shock Incident — Splash in the Boro Waterpark, Statesboro, GA

## Summary

On May 26, 2025 (Memorial Day), approximately 5 patrons including at least 2 children reported feeling electric shocks while in the wave pool at Splash in the Boro Waterpark in Statesboro, Bulloch County, Georgia. The pool was evacuated at approximately 1:30 PM. Statesboro Fire Department responded and classified the incident as "arcing, shorted electrical equipment," identifying a blower unit that potentially shorted out or overheated. EMS evaluated those affected; none were hospitalized. The wave pool was closed and subsequently inspected by 5 licensed electricians and Georgia Power before reopening several days later.
