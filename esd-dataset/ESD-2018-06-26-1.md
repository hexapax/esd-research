# ESD-2018-06-26-1: Zynae Green — MGM National Harbor Fountain, Oxon Hill MD

## Summary

On June 26, 2018, 6-year-old Zynae Green was severely injured by electric shock at the Potomac Plaza outdoor decorative fountain area at MGM National Harbor casino/resort in Oxon Hill, Maryland. An illuminated metal handrail near the fountain had been energized with 120V AC after an LED driver (designed to step 120V down to 12V) failed due to improper installation and faulty wiring. Zynae suffered massive traumatic brain injury and cardiac arrest, leaving her paralyzed and unable to walk or talk. Her 5-year-old brother, 16-year-old sister, mother Rydricka Rosier, and an MGM security guard were also injured. Surveillance video later revealed that other patrons had been jolted by the same handrail in the days BEFORE Zynae's incident — a prior-knowledge failure pattern mirroring the Jupiter FL Harbourside Place fountain case. MGM National Harbor and its contractors reached an undisclosed settlement with the Green family in December 2019.

## Key Facts

- **Venue:** MGM National Harbor casino/resort, Potomac Plaza outdoor fountain area
- **Electrical source:** Illuminated metal handrail adjacent to the fountain (LED driver failure; 120V→12V step-down failed, energizing rail with full line voltage)
- **Severity:** Severe permanent disability (non-fatal); 4 additional injuries
- **Prior knowledge:** Surveillance video showed other patrons jolted in days before the severe incident
- **Resolution:** Undisclosed settlement, December 2019

## Hazard Category Note

This incident involves a distinct mechanism from classic dock/marina ESD and from typical in-water fountain electrocutions. The electrical path was through a metal handrail adjacent to the fountain (not through water-submerged light fixtures), and the fault mechanism was an LED driver failure — a component class increasingly common in modern decorative-water installations. The case should be analyzed alongside fountain incidents but flagged for the LED driver / low-voltage lighting failure subcategory.
