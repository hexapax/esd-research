# ESD-2019-06-27-1: Roy Charles Stoltz — El Paso, TX

## Summary

On the evening of June 27, 2019, 50-year-old Roy Charles Stoltz was electrocuted in the swimming pool at the Hallmark Apartments complex at 1620 Mescalero Drive in El Paso, Texas. The pool's lights activated automatically at dusk and sent electrical current into the water, electrocuting Stoltz and shocking multiple other guests who were in the pool. A friend performed CPR on Stoltz; he was transported to a hospital, where he died. The pool had passed a City of El Paso inspection just two days earlier, on June 25, 2019 — a stark illustration that routine regulatory inspections can fail to catch fatal electrical faults. The incident was covered by the El Paso Times, KTSM, CBS4 El Paso, and Fox San Antonio.
