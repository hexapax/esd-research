# ESD-2025-08-25-1: Family Electrocuted in Backyard Pool — Logansport, IN

## Summary

On August 25, 2025, five people — two adults and three juveniles — were electrocuted in a backyard swimming pool at a private residence on the 2600 block of High Street in Logansport, Cass County, Indiana. A wire for the pool's submersible pump had become pinched near the pool ladder, causing the protective cover to break and exposing bare wire to the water. Swimmers felt "a vibration in the water" before the father yelled to shut the pump off. A 15-year-old boy managed to jump out of the pool once power was cut and heroically rescued his father, sister, and sister's friend. Two of the five victims were unresponsive when EMTs arrived. All five were hospitalized. Police Chief Travis Yike described it as a "freak accident."
