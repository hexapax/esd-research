# ESD-2022-00-00-1: Jaelyn Velez — Downtown Palm Beach Gardens Splash Pad, FL

## Summary

In approximately 2022, Jaelyn Velez, age 12, was electrically shocked at the Downtown Palm Beach Gardens splash pad in Palm Beach County, Florida. According to her mother Rochelle Kus, Velez waded through the public splash pad and touched a nearby directory sign, receiving what the subsequent lawsuit described as "a serious electrocution injury." The incident came to public attention in November 2023 after the fatal Harbourside Place fountain electrocution in nearby Jupiter prompted Kus to file suit and speak publicly. The case highlights a significant regulatory gap: while the National Electrical Code requires GFCI protection in fountains and splash pads at initial construction, Florida does not mandate periodic electrical inspections of these facilities — only water quality inspections are required.
